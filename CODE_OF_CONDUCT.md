# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to making the DeskMesh community welcoming, safe, and equitable. Everyone participating in good faith receives the same opportunity to contribute. We respect each person's dignity, rights, identity, background, experience, language, beliefs, and contributions.

## Encouraged behavior

Community members should:

1. Respect the purpose of the project and the boundaries of other people.
2. Communicate kindly, honestly, and constructively.
3. Welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience.
4. Accept responsibility for their actions and repair harm where possible.
5. Give credit, protect confidential information, and prioritize community well-being.

## Restricted behavior

Harassment, character attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexualized conduct, disclosure of private information, threats, impersonation, plagiarism, spam, and other behavior that endangers the community are not permitted. Repeated unwanted attention after a clear request to stop is also a violation.

## Reporting

Report possible violations privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/bz1121/DeskMesh/security/advisories/new), with `Code of Conduct` at the start of the title. If the form is unavailable, contact [@bz1121](https://github.com/bz1121) using a private method shown on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not expose affected people or incident details in a public Issue.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and prioritize the safety of affected people. Depending on severity and history, responses may include a private warning, temporary limits, suspension, or a permanent ban. Serious cases may skip earlier steps. Enforcement discussions are private unless disclosure is needed for safety or agreed by those affected.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone officially represents DeskMesh in public or private community spaces.
